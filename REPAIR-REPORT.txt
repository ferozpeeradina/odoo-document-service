Precise remaining gaps and limitations

Tamper reads were tested at the authenticated provider service boundary inside
the Odoo shell, using rollback-only storage mutations. HTTP tamper responses
during committed corruption are NOT TESTED. Ordinary HTTP access/retrieval and
restart checks were actually run. Rewriting both database bytes and integrity
anchors by an administrator is not prevented or independently detected.
Application database SHA-256 hashes and OCA signature images/audit records are
not an independently sealed cryptographic PDF signature or trusted timestamp.
Synthetic local authenticated accounts do not establish mailbox verification,
OTP, real human identity, legal enforceability or production identity assurance.
Concurrent operation races, crash recovery, backup restore, load/throughput,
production DMS, real customer data, live providers and broader route/tenant
penetration testing are NOT TESTED. No production rollout is approved here.
